# 30-Second Drills — Rapid-Fire Concept Recall

> *For the commute. For the gym. For the night before an interview. Read aloud. Set a 30-second timer per item. If you cannot give a complete answer in 30 seconds, you don't own that concept yet — go back to the module.*

Source: drills curated from [Module 17.10](../modules/17-practice-scenarios.md), expanded across the curriculum.

---

## Foundations (Modules 0–4)

1. Define **security**, **privacy**, **compliance**, and **GRC** in one sentence each.
2. Name the **CIA triad** and one real-world example of each being violated.
3. **Authentication vs authorisation** — what's the order, and why does it matter?
4. **Three factors of authentication.** Give an example of each.
5. Explain **Multi-Factor Authentication** and why "two passwords" doesn't qualify.
6. **Encoding vs encryption** — why is the distinction career-saving?
7. Walk through a **TLS 1.3 handshake** in plain English.
8. **DNS lookup** in three sentences.
9. **STRIDE** — name the six categories and one example each.
10. **Defence in depth** — define and give a 3-layer example.

## A senior's check

If you can complete all 85 in two sittings, with no prompts, in roughly 30 seconds each, you are at junior-to-mid interview-ready level for GRC and privacy, and at strong-junior level for technical security roles.

If you stumble on 20+, you have a focused gap. Go back to the modules whose drills you missed. The drills are the cheapest possible diagnostic.
