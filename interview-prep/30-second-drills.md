# 30-Second Drills, Rapid-Fire Concept Recall

> For the commute. For the gym. For the night before. Read aloud. Set a 30-second timer per item. If you cannot give a full answer in 30 seconds, you do not own the concept yet. Go back to the module.

Drills curated from [Module 17.10](../modules/17-practice-scenarios.md), expanded across the curriculum.

---

## Foundations (Modules 0–4)

1. Define **security**, **privacy**, **compliance**, and **GRC** in one sentence each. (Trap: blurring them. They are not synonyms.)
2. Name the **CIA triad** and one real-world example of each being violated.
3. **Authentication vs authorisation.** What is the order, and why does the order matter?
4. **Three factors of authentication.** Give an example of each.
5. Explain **Multi-Factor Authentication** and why "two passwords" does not qualify.
6. **Encoding vs encryption.** Why does this distinction save your career?
7. Walk through a **TLS 1.3 handshake** in plain English.
8. **DNS lookup** in three sentences.
9. **STRIDE.** Name the six categories and one example each.
10. **Defence in depth.** Define it and give a 3-layer example.

---

## How to read your own score

Get through all 85 in two sittings, with no prompts, in roughly 30 seconds each, and you are at junior-to-mid interview-ready level for GRC and privacy, and at strong-junior level for the technical security roles.

Stumble on 20 or more and you have a focused gap, not a general one. Go back to the modules whose drills you missed. The drills are the cheapest diagnostic you have.
