# India Market Snapshot — 2025–26

> *An honest read on the Indian security, privacy, and GRC job market: who hires, what they pay, what they actually want. Source: [Module 16.6](../modules/16-careers-and-interviews.md), with current-year context.*

**Last verified:** 17 April 2026.

---

## Who hires

### Big 4 (Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG)

- The largest GRC and IT-audit recruiter in India.
- Entry roles: **Analyst** (campus + lateral) → **Associate** → **Senior Associate** → **Manager** → **Senior Manager** → **Director / Partner**.
- Strong bias for **CA, MBA, or CISA** at lateral entry.
- Broad client exposure across industries; you'll touch SOC 2, ISO 27001, SOX IT, RBI/SEBI/IRDAI work in your first two years.
- Churn is real: 25–35% annual attrition typical. People leave for in-house GRC roles after 2–3 years.

**Honest take:** the best place to learn breadth fast. Stay 18–36 months, build the resume, exit to in-house at a 30–50% comp jump.

### Product SaaS (Zoho, Freshworks, Postman, Razorpay, CRED, Atlassian India, Browserstack, Hasura, Chargebee, etc.)

- Lean GRC teams (2–8 people typically).
- Hire **generalists** who can do GRC + a bit of privacy + a bit of TPRM. Bonus if you can read code.
- Strong bias for self-starters; less hand-holding than Big 4.
- Equity often part of comp.

**Honest take:** best for career-shifters who want fast growth and ownership. Bring portfolio artefacts; certs matter less than capability shown.

### BFSI (banks, NBFCs, insurers)

- Heavily regulated by **RBI, IRDAI, SEBI**.
- Demand: heavy on **internal audits, vendor risk, ITGCs (IT General Controls), CERT-In + RBI cyber-incident reporting**.
- Pay good, tech pace slower than SaaS, hierarchy more visible.
- CISA, ISO LA, CRISC, CISM go a long way here.

**Honest take:** stable, well-paying, defines a clear ladder. Less Silicon-Valley culture; more banking culture.

### Global Capability Centres (GCCs / in-house centres for MNCs)

- Often the best comp for tech security roles (AppSec, cloud security, detection engineering).
- Examples: Walmart Global Tech, Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan, Wells Fargo, Target, Lowe's, ANZ, Standard Chartered, Visa, Mastercard, Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Adobe.
- Work feeds the global parent; you're contributing to global tooling and IR.
- Better technical depth than most Indian product SaaS.

**Honest take:** if your goal is technical security engineering at scale, this is the bracket to target.

### Consulting boutiques (DSCI-empanelled, ISO firms)

- Examples: SISA, Lucideus / SAFE Security, NetSPI India, KPMG/EY/Deloitte sub-practices, Network Intelligence, eSec Forte, DSCI-empanelled firms.
- Fast exposure to multiple clients; great for getting **ISO 27001 LI / LA experience**.
- Pay lower than GCCs at the same level; experience earned faster.

**Honest take:** good 2–4 year stint to build the portfolio; rarely a long-term home.

### Startups (1st security hire)

- You wear every hat: GRC + AppSec + IR + vendor risk + privacy.
- High learning rate, high ambiguity, high responsibility.
- Equity matters more than salary; do diligence on the cap table.

**Honest take:** a great accelerant if the founder respects security; a nightmare if you're hired as a checkbox.

- Big 4 pay below product SaaS at junior levels but ramps faster with title.

## Most employable certification combos in India

From [Module 16.2](../modules/16-careers-and-interviews.md), what we see actually working in 2025–26:

- **Entry GRC/Audit:** ISC2 CC (free) + Security+ + ISO 27001 Lead Implementer.
- **GRC career builder (best ROI in India):** **ISO 27001 LI + CISA**. This combo opens Big 4, GCCs, and product SaaS.
- **Privacy specialist:** CIPP/E + CDPSE, or CIPM if operational lean.
- **AppSec / Cloud:** Security+ + AWS Security Specialty (or SC-200 / GCP Pro Cloud Security) + optionally OSCP if offensive-leaning.
- **Senior IC / management:** add CISM, CRISC, or CISSP after 5+ years' experience.

**Avoid:** stacking 5 certs without a portfolio. Hiring managers see through it.

## Where the demand is growing fastest in 2026

1. **DPDP operationalisation** — Significant Data Fiduciaries staffing up DPO offices, consent-management integrations, DPIA cadence.
2. **AI security** — anyone shipping LLM features needs threat-modelling, OWASP LLM Top 10 awareness, EU AI Act categorisation.
3. **Cloud security engineering** — CSPM/CWPP/CIEM specialists, multi-cloud landing-zone designers.
4. **TPRM at scale** — vendor questionnaire response, Trust Center operations, sub-processor governance.
5. **GRC engineering** — automating evidence collection from CI/CD, IaC scanning, control-as-code.
6. **Privacy engineering** — DSR automation, data-flow mapping tools, privacy-by-design integration.

---

## Where demand is softening

- Pure manual SOC L1 alert triage (being automated by SOAR/AI).
- Pure documentation-only GRC roles (being automated by Vanta/Drata-style platforms).
- Generic "cybersecurity awareness trainer" without a specialism.

The hedge: pair your GRC/privacy work with one technical capability (cloud, AI, or automation). The combination is what stays employable.

## How to find roles (channels that actually work in India)

1. **LinkedIn Jobs** — set alerts; apply within 48 hours of posting.
2. **Direct on company careers pages** — ATS bias rewards directness.
3. **Recruiter networks** — Antal, Michael Page, Randstad, Vahan for tech security.
4. **DSCI events and CERT-In conclaves** — for in-person introductions.
5. **NULL community + OWASP India chapters + Bsides India** — for technical roles.
6. **Big 4 referrals** — current employees get bonus; you get a faster pipeline.

LinkedIn-pinned portfolio + 5–10 substantive posts on real regulatory events outperform sending 200 cold applications. See [Module 16.3](../modules/16-careers-and-interviews.md) on portfolio construction.

## A senior's read on the market in two paragraphs

The Indian market is in transition. DPDP operationalisation, EU AI Act extraterritorial impact, and CERT-In's tightened reporting regime are creating real demand for people who can translate regulation into engineering and operational decisions. This is exactly the GRC-plus-technical-literacy profile this curriculum builds. Pure paper-pushing roles are being squeezed by GRC platforms; pure tools-only roles are being squeezed by automation. The middle — **people who understand both controls and code, both regulators and engineers** — is widening.

If you arrive in 2026 with a real portfolio (risk register, DPIA, SoA, IR runbook, vendor assessment) and one technical capability (cloud or AI fluency), and the right cert combo (Sec+ + ISO LI + CISA for GRC; AWS Security or SC-200 for tech), you will not struggle for interviews. The struggle will be choosing between offers, not getting them.
