# India Market Snapshot, 2025–26

> An honest read on the Indian security, privacy and GRC job market. Who hires, what they pay, what they actually want. Source: [Module 16.6](../modules/16-careers-and-interviews.md), with current-year context.

**Last verified:** 17 April 2026.

---

## Who hires

### Big 4 (Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG)

- The largest GRC and IT-audit recruiter in India.
- Entry ladder: **Analyst** (campus and lateral) → **Associate** → **Senior Associate** → **Manager** → **Senior Manager** → **Director / Partner**.
- Strong bias for **CA, MBA, or CISA** at lateral entry.
- Broad client exposure. You touch SOC 2, ISO 27001, SOX IT, and RBI/SEBI/IRDAI work in the first two years.
- Churn is real. Annual attrition of 25–35% is typical. Most leave for in-house GRC after 2–3 years.

Honest take: the best place to pick up breadth fast. Stay 18–36 months, build the CV, exit to in-house at a 30–50% comp jump.

### Product SaaS (Zoho, Freshworks, Postman, Razorpay, CRED, Atlassian India, Browserstack, Hasura, Chargebee, and similar)

- Lean GRC teams. Usually 2–8 people.
- Hire **generalists** who can do GRC plus a bit of privacy plus a bit of TPRM. Bonus if you can read code.
- Less hand-holding than Big 4. You are expected to figure things out.
- Equity is usually part of the package.

Honest take: best for career-shifters who want fast growth and real ownership. Portfolio artefacts matter more than certs.

### BFSI (banks, NBFCs, insurers)

- Heavily regulated by **RBI, IRDAI, SEBI**.
- Workload is heavy on **internal audits, vendor risk, ITGCs, CERT-In and RBI cyber-incident reporting**.
- Pay is good. Tech pace is slower than SaaS. Hierarchy is visible.
- CISA, ISO LA, CRISC and CISM go a long way here.

Honest take: stable, well-paying, clear ladder. Less Silicon-Valley culture, more banking culture.

### Global Capability Centres (GCCs)

- Often the best comp for technical security roles (AppSec, cloud security, detection engineering).
- Examples: Walmart Global Tech, Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan, Wells Fargo, Target, Lowe's, ANZ, Standard Chartered, Visa, Mastercard, Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Adobe.
- Work feeds the global parent. You contribute to global tooling and global IR.
- Technical depth is usually better than Indian product SaaS.

Honest take: if your goal is technical security engineering at scale, target this bracket.

### Consulting boutiques (DSCI-empanelled, ISO firms)

- Examples: SISA, Lucideus / SAFE Security, NetSPI India, Big 4 sub-practices, Network Intelligence, eSec Forte, other DSCI-empanelled firms.
- Fast exposure across multiple clients. Good for getting **ISO 27001 LI / LA experience**.
- Pay lower than GCCs at the same level. Experience accrues faster.

Honest take: a good 2–4 year stint to build the portfolio. Rarely a long-term home.

### Startups (first security hire)

- You wear every hat. GRC plus AppSec plus IR plus vendor risk plus privacy.
- High learning rate, high ambiguity, high responsibility.
- Equity matters more than salary. Do diligence on the cap table.

Honest take: an accelerant if the founder takes security seriously. A nightmare if you have been hired as a checkbox for an upcoming SOC 2.

- Big 4 pay sits below product SaaS at junior levels but ramps faster with title.

## Cert combos that actually work in India

From [Module 16.2](../modules/16-careers-and-interviews.md), what we see hire in 2025–26:

- **Entry GRC / Audit:** ISC2 CC (free) + Security+ + ISO 27001 Lead Implementer.
- **GRC career builder (best ROI in India):** **ISO 27001 LI + CISA**. This combo opens Big 4, GCCs and product SaaS.
- **Privacy specialist:** CIPP/E + CDPSE, or CIPM if you lean operational.
- **AppSec / Cloud:** Security+ + AWS Security Specialty (or SC-200 / GCP Pro Cloud Security), plus OSCP if you lean offensive.
- **Senior IC or management:** add CISM, CRISC, or CISSP after 5+ years of experience.

Avoid: stacking five certs without a portfolio. Hiring managers see through it. I have personally interviewed candidates with four certs and no story to tell about any of them. They do not get past the second round.

## Where demand is growing fastest in 2026

1. **DPDP operationalisation.** Significant Data Fiduciaries staffing up DPO offices, consent-management integrations, DPIA cadence.
2. **AI security.** Anyone shipping LLM features needs threat modelling, OWASP LLM Top 10 awareness, and EU AI Act categorisation.
3. **Cloud security engineering.** CSPM/CWPP/CIEM specialists, multi-cloud landing-zone designers.
4. **TPRM at scale.** Vendor questionnaire response, Trust Center operations, sub-processor governance.
5. **GRC engineering.** Automating evidence collection from CI/CD, IaC scanning, control-as-code.
6. **Privacy engineering.** DSR automation, data-flow mapping tools, privacy-by-design integration.

---

## Where demand is softening

- Pure manual SOC L1 alert triage. Being automated by SOAR and AI.
- Pure documentation-only GRC roles. Being automated by Vanta/Drata-style platforms.
- Generic "cybersecurity awareness trainer" without a specialism.

The hedge: pair your GRC or privacy work with one technical capability. Cloud, AI, or automation. The combination is what stays employable.

## Channels that actually land roles in India

1. **LinkedIn Jobs.** Set alerts. Apply within 48 hours of posting.
2. **Direct on company careers pages.** ATS bias rewards directness.
3. **Recruiter networks.** Antal, Michael Page, Randstad, Vahan for tech security.
4. **DSCI events and CERT-In conclaves.** For in-person introductions.
5. **NULL community, OWASP India chapters, BSides India.** For technical roles.
6. **Big 4 referrals.** Current employees get bonus. You get a faster pipeline.

A pinned portfolio on LinkedIn plus 5–10 substantive posts on real regulatory events outperforms 200 cold applications. See [Module 16.3](../modules/16-careers-and-interviews.md) on portfolio construction.

## A senior read on the market in two paragraphs

The Indian market is in transition. DPDP operationalisation, EU AI Act extraterritorial impact, and CERT-In's tightened reporting regime are creating real demand for people who can translate regulation into engineering and operational decisions. This is the GRC-plus-technical-literacy profile this curriculum builds. Paper-pushing roles are being squeezed by GRC platforms. Tools-only roles are being squeezed by automation. The middle, people who understand both controls and code, both regulators and engineers, is widening.

If you arrive in 2026 with a real portfolio (risk register, DPIA, SoA, IR runbook, vendor assessment) and one technical capability (cloud or AI fluency), and the right cert combo (Sec+ plus ISO LI plus CISA for GRC; AWS Security or SC-200 for tech), you will not struggle for interviews. The struggle will be choosing between offers, not getting them.
