# Path 🟡 — Career-Shifter

> *You have professional experience — typically in finance, audit, law, project management, operations, HR, customer success, or IT support — and you want to move into security, privacy, or GRC as a real career. You bring transferable strengths: writing, stakeholder management, process discipline, audit literacy, regulatory comfort. What you need is the domain language and the artefacts.*

**Estimated time:** 140–180 hours over 16–20 weeks (8–10 hours per week).
**End state:** ready to apply for **Senior GRC Analyst**, **Privacy Manager**, **IT Auditor**, **Compliance Manager**, **TPRM Lead**, or **Security Awareness Lead** roles, with a portfolio that demonstrates depth.

## How your background actually maps

| Coming from | Maps strongly to | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Audit (statutory / internal)** | IT Audit, GRC, ISO LA, SOX IT | Evidence-collection muscle, working-paper discipline |
| **Finance / Accounting** | SOX, SOC 1, BFSI compliance, FAIR-style risk | ICFR vocabulary, materiality thinking |
| **Law / Paralegal** | Privacy (DPO/DPM), Trust & Safety, Policy | Statutory interpretation, drafting precision |
| **Project / Programme Mgmt** | GRC programme lead, Audit lead, BCP | Stakeholder + timeline + dependency thinking |
| **Customer Success / Support** | TPRM, Trust & Safety, GRC, Privacy ops | Communication + escalation discipline |
| **HR** | Insider risk, awareness, BCP, policy | People-process fluency |
| **IT Support / Helpdesk** | SOC L1, IAM Ops, GRC, vendor risk | Existing systems intuition |
| **Operations / BPO** | Process risk, SOC 2 controls, vendor mgmt | SOP and control-design instinct |

Lean into your background in interviews. The candidate who says "I led 8 statutory audits and I want to apply that evidence discipline to ISO 27001" is more credible than the one who says "I just did the Google Cybersecurity Certificate."

## Your portfolio target by week 18

Per [Module 16.3](../modules/16-careers-and-interviews.md), your GitHub or Notion should hold:

- **A risk register** with 15+ rows, scored, owners assigned, treatments documented.
- **A DPIA** for the AI feature scenario (Scenario 7) or the SDF scenario (Scenario 6).
- **A Statement of Applicability** covering at least 20 ISO 27001:2022 Annex A controls.
- **An IR runbook** for one playbook of your choice.
- **A vendor assessment** with tiering, DDQ response, and a 2-page memo with findings.
- **A policy set** (3 policies, 1 standard, 1 procedure) mapped to ISO 27001 and SOC 2.
- **A cross-mapping spreadsheet** (12 rows, 5 frameworks).
- **5–10 LinkedIn write-ups** on real regulatory events: a CERT-In directive, a public breach, the DPDP Rules, an EU AI Act milestone.

This is **already** more demonstrable than what most 2-year GRC analysts have. Use it.

## Certification path (in order, only as you can afford / time)

1. **ISC2 CC** — free. Get it in week 4.
2. **CompTIA Security+** — paid (~$370). Take after Module 7. Optional but widely recognised.
3. **ISO 27001:2022 Lead Implementer** (PECB or BSI) — most useful single GRC cert in India and EMEA. Take after Module 10.
4. **CISA** (ISACA) — gold standard for IT audit. Take 6–12 months in. Required at Big 4.
5. **CIPP/E** (IAPP) — if your trajectory is privacy-heavy. Take after Module 6 mastery.
6. **CRISC, CISM, CISSP** — later. They are ceiling-raisers, not starter certs.

Per [Module 16.2](../modules/16-careers-and-interviews.md): in India, **ISO 27001 LI + CISA** is the most employable GRC combo at Big 4, GCCs, and SaaS companies.

## Salary expectation, honestly

India 2025–26, INR LPA, see [interview-prep/india-market-snapshot.md](../interview-prep/india-market-snapshot.md):

- Mid-career switcher landing first GRC role: **8–15** (with relevant experience leverage).
- After 18 months and an ISO LI + CISA combo: **15–25**.
- Privacy Manager / Senior GRC at 4–6 years total experience: **18–35**.

Remote-first global roles often offer 1.8–3× India-local bands. Many career-switchers lift their compensation faster than they expect once the certs and portfolio are in place.

## One mindset shift before you begin

You are not "starting over." You are **redeploying** evidence skills, audit instincts, and stakeholder fluency into a domain with new vocabulary and tighter clocks. Treat this curriculum as the vocabulary acquisition, not a personality change. Your strongest interview answers will start: *"In my previous role I handled X, and I'd apply the same approach here by…"*
