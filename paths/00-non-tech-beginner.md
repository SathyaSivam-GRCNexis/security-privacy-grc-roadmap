# Path 🟢 — Non-Tech Beginner

> *You are exploring whether security, privacy, or GRC is for you. You have no IT background, and that is fine. Many of the best practitioners came from law, audit, customer success, journalism, social sciences, or unrelated fields.*

**Estimated time:** 80–100 hours over 10–14 weeks (8–10 hours per week).
**End state:** ready to apply for **GRC Analyst**, **Privacy Analyst**, **Compliance Associate**, **TPRM Analyst**, or **IT Audit Associate** roles.

## What you can skip (and what you cannot)

You can skim or skip:

- **Module 02 — Cryptography** beyond the "what each algorithm is for" table. You don't need to implement crypto.
- **Module 04 — Network Security** beyond the basics in Module 0.2. Read for vocabulary, don't memorise.
- **Module 13 — Cloud Security** beyond the shared-responsibility model. Come back to it later.
- **Module 14 — AppSec** beyond OWASP Top 10 awareness. You won't be reviewing code on day one.
- **Module 15 — Emerging Topics** until you have a job. Read it for interview talking points; don't try to master AI security on week two.

You **cannot** skip and you must do well:

- **Module 0** — the entire foundation pack.
- **Module 1** — security first principles (CIA, threat modelling vocabulary).
- **Module 3** — identity and access (most of GRC sits on top of IAM).
- **Modules 5–10** — privacy fundamentals through policies. **This is your core.**
- **Module 16** — careers and interviews.
- **Module 17** — pick at least 3 scenarios from 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 9 and do them properly.

## What success looks like at the end

You can:

- Define security, privacy, compliance, and GRC without confusing them.
- Read a SOC 2 Type II report and find the actual signal in Section 4.
- Walk through a SOC 2 audit lifecycle from kickoff to report.
- Map a control to SOC 2, ISO 27001:2022 Annex A, and NIST CSF 2.0.
- Respond to a GDPR Article 17 deletion request without panicking.
- Explain the DPDP Act SDF obligations to a fintech CTO.
- Build and defend a 10-row risk register.
- Write a one-page access-control policy that an auditor would accept.
- Discuss two completed capstone scenarios in an interview, not recite them.

That is a junior GRC analyst's interview-day skillset. You are now hireable.

## Two warnings before you start

**One.** Do not buy certifications before you have done the work. The free **ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC)** is enough as a first credential. Save the money for a **CIPP/E** or **ISO 27001 Lead Implementer** *after* you have a job offer or a clear interview pipeline.

**Two.** Do not chase tools. GRC tools (Vanta, Drata, Secureframe, OneTrust) are easy to learn once you understand the controls. Learn the controls first; the tools are a one-week onboarding.
