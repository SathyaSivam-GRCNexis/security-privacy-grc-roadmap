# Appendix A: Free Resources Master List

> Everything referenced across the course, plus the extras I actually open in a normal week. Legend: 🏛 official / standards · 📘 free course · 📰 article / blog / newsletter · 🎥 video / channel · 🧪 hands-on lab · 💰 paid (only listed where the free option is genuinely weaker).

Every link below has a clear free path unless marked 💰. Where vendor content sneaks in dressed as education, I have flagged it.

## A.9 How to use this list without burning out

A few habits that actually worked for me, after trying most of the wrong ones:

1. **Pick a weekly rhythm.** One newsletter, one video, one lab a week beats ten half-read bookmarks. Always.
2. **Keep a notes file.** One line per thing you learned, linked to its source. Future-you will thank present-you when answering a SIG question.
3. **Teach it forward.** Post a five-line explainer on LinkedIn after every module. It reinforces the learning and quietly builds a portfolio.
4. **Cycle the focus.** Two weeks technical, two weeks GRC and privacy, two weeks emerging topics. Repeat.
5. **Do not chase certs first.** Two well-chosen certs plus a portfolio beats a wall of badges every time. Hiring managers can tell the difference.

That is a free curriculum that holds up against most paid bootcamps. The rest is reps.
