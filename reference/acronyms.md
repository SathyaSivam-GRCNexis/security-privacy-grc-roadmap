# Acronym Zoo: Decoded

> *GRC runs on acronyms. Half my first year was quietly Googling them in meetings. This is the cheat sheet I wish I had.*

**Last verified:** 17 April 2026. Sourced from the glossary recaps of all 18 modules.

Quick tip: when an auditor or a customer uses an acronym you do not recognise, ask. Nobody wins points for nodding through a SIG response and then mis-mapping CC6.1 to the wrong control.

---

## A

- **ABAC**: Attribute-Based Access Control
- **AICPA**: American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (publishes SOC criteria)
- **ALE**: Annualised Loss Expectancy (= SLE × ARO)
- **ANPD**: Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (Brazil's privacy regulator)
- **APP**: Australian Privacy Principles
- **ARO**: Annualised Rate of Occurrence
- **ASD**: Australian Signals Directorate
- **ATO**: Authority to Operate (US federal RMF authorisation)
- **ATT&CK**: Adversarial Tactics, Techniques & Common Knowledge (MITRE)

## B

- **BAA**: Business Associate Agreement (HIPAA)
- **BCP**: Business Continuity Plan
- **BCR**: Binding Corporate Rules (GDPR transfer mechanism)
- **BEC**: Business Email Compromise
- **BIA**: Business Impact Analysis
- **BYOK**: Bring Your Own Key

## C

- **C5**: Cloud Computing Compliance Criteria Catalogue (Germany)
- **CA**: Certificate Authority
- **CAC**: Cyberspace Administration of China
- **CAIQ**: Consensus Assessments Initiative Questionnaire (CSA)
- **CC**: Common Criteria (also: ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity)
- **CCM**: Cloud Controls Matrix (CSA)
- **CCPA / CPRA**: California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act
- **CCSP**: Certified Cloud Security Professional (ISC2)
- **CDE**: Cardholder Data Environment (PCI)
- **CDPSE**: Certified Data Privacy Solutions Engineer (ISACA)
- **CERT-In**: Indian Computer Emergency Response Team
- **CIA**: Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability
- **CIPM**: Certified Information Privacy Manager (IAPP)
- **CIPP/E**: Certified Information Privacy Professional, Europe (IAPP)
- **CIPT**: Certified Information Privacy Technologist (IAPP)
- **CIS**: Center for Internet Security
- **CISA**: Certified Information Systems Auditor (ISACA); also US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (the two get confused constantly in conversation; clarify which one early)
- **CISM**: Certified Information Security Manager (ISACA)
- **CISO**: Chief Information Security Officer
- **CISSP**: Certified Information Systems Security Professional (ISC2)
- **CMDB**: Configuration Management Database
- **CMMC**: Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (US DoD)
- **COBIT**: Control Objectives for Information and Related Technologies (ISACA)
- **COPPA**: Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (US)
- **COSO**: Committee of Sponsoring Organizations
- **CPA**: Certified Public Accountant; also Colorado Privacy Act (the accountant version is what shows up in SOC report signature blocks)
- **CPPA**: California Privacy Protection Agency
- **CRISC**: Certified in Risk and Information Systems Control (ISACA)
- **CRO**: Chief Risk Officer
- **CSA**: Cloud Security Alliance
- **CSCRF**: Cyber Security and Cyber Resilience Framework (SEBI)
- **CSF**: Cybersecurity Framework (NIST)
- **CSIRT**: Computer Security Incident Response Team
- **CSPM**: Cloud Security Posture Management
- **CTDPA**: Connecticut Data Privacy Act
- **CUI**: Controlled Unclassified Information
- **CVE**: Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures

## D

- **DDoS**: Distributed Denial of Service
- **DDQ**: Due Diligence Questionnaire
- **DEK**: Data Encryption Key
- **DFIR**: Digital Forensics and Incident Response
- **DLP**: Data Leakage / Loss Prevention
- **DMARC**: Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting and Conformance
- **DNS**: Domain Name System
- **DORA**: Digital Operational Resilience Act (EU financial)
- **DPA**: Data Processing Agreement; also Data Protection Authority (context tells you which; in a vendor email it is almost always the contract)
- **DPBI**: Data Protection Board of India (DPDP regulator)
- **DPDP**: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (India)
- **DPIA**: Data Protection Impact Assessment
- **DPO**: Data Protection Officer
- **DR**: Disaster Recovery
- **DSCI**: Data Security Council of India
- **DSR / DSAR**: Data Subject Request / Data Subject Access Request

## E

- **EDPB**: European Data Protection Board
- **EDR**: Endpoint Detection and Response
- **ENISA**: European Union Agency for Cybersecurity
- **ERM**: Enterprise Risk Management

## F

- **FAIR**: Factor Analysis of Information Risk
- **FedRAMP**: Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program (US)
- **FERPA**: Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (US)
- **FIDO2**: Fast Identity Online v2 (passkey standard)
- **FIPS**: Federal Information Processing Standard

## G

- **GCC**: Global Capability Centre (in-house centre for MNCs in India)
- **GDPR**: General Data Protection Regulation (EU)
- **GIAC**: Global Information Assurance Certification (SANS)
- **GLBA**: Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (US financial)
- **GPC**: Global Privacy Control (CCPA opt-out signal)
- **GRC**: Governance, Risk, Compliance

## H

- **HDS**: Hébergeur de Données de Santé (French health data hosting)
- **HHS OCR**: US Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights
- **HIPAA**: Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (US)
- **HITRUST**: Health Information Trust Alliance (CSF)
- **HSM**: Hardware Security Module
- **HYOK**: Hold Your Own Key

## I

- **IAM**: Identity and Access Management
- **IAPP**: International Association of Privacy Professionals
- **ICFR**: Internal Controls over Financial Reporting (SOC 1 / SOX)
- **ICO**: Information Commissioner's Office (UK)
- **IDOR**: Insecure Direct Object Reference
- **IDS / IPS**: Intrusion Detection / Prevention System
- **IIA**: Institute of Internal Auditors
- **IR**: Incident Response
- **IRAP**: Information Security Registered Assessors Program (Australia)
- **IRDAI**: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India
- **ISACA**: Information Systems Audit and Control Association
- **ISC2**: International Information System Security Certification Consortium
- **ISMAP**: Information system Security Management and Assessment Program (Japan)
- **ISMS**: Information Security Management System (ISO 27001)

## K

- **KEK**: Key Encryption Key
- **KMS**: Key Management Service
- **KPI / KRI**: Key Performance / Risk Indicator
- **KYC**: Know Your Customer

## L

- **LGPD**: Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (Brazil)
- **LLM**: Large Language Model

## M

- **MDM**: Mobile Device Management
- **MDR**: Managed Detection and Response
- **MFA**: Multi-Factor Authentication
- **MITM**: Man-in-the-Middle
- **MTD**: Maximum Tolerable Downtime
- **MTTD / MTTR**: Mean Time to Detect / Respond (or Recover)

## N

- **NC / OFI**: Non-Conformity / Opportunity for Improvement (audit terminology)
- **NHA / ABDM**: National Health Authority / Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (India)
- **NICE**: National Initiative for Cybersecurity Education (NIST workforce framework)
- **NIS2**: Network and Information Security Directive 2 (EU)
- **NIST**: National Institute of Standards and Technology (US)

## O

- **OCPA**: Oregon Consumer Privacy Act
- **OFAC**: Office of Foreign Assets Control (US sanctions)
- **OIDC**: OpenID Connect
- **OSCP**: Offensive Security Certified Professional
- **OWASP**: Open Worldwide Application Security Project

## P

- **PCI-DSS**: Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard
- **PDPA**: Personal Data Protection Act (Singapore / Thailand variants)
- **PDPC**: Personal Data Protection Commission (Singapore)
- **PETs**: Privacy-Enhancing Technologies
- **PHI**: Protected Health Information (HIPAA)
- **PII**: Personally Identifiable Information
- **PIM**: Privileged Identity Management
- **PIPEDA**: Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (Canada)
- **PIPL**: Personal Information Protection Law (China)
- **POPIA**: Protection of Personal Information Act (South Africa)
- **PQC**: Post-Quantum Cryptography
- **PR.AA / PR.DS / etc.**: NIST CSF 2.0 subcategory codes

## R

- **RACI**: Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed
- **RBAC**: Role-Based Access Control
- **RBI**: Reserve Bank of India
- **ReBAC**: Relationship-Based Access Control
- **RMF**: Risk Management Framework (NIST 800-37)
- **ROC**: Report on Compliance (PCI)
- **ROPA**: Records of Processing Activities (GDPR Art. 30) (auditors actually ask to see this; keep it current, not just at audit time)
- **RPO / RTO**: Recovery Point / Time Objective

## S

- **SAML**: Security Assertion Markup Language
- **SAQ**: Self-Assessment Questionnaire (PCI: A, A-EP, B, B-IP, C, C-VT, D, P2PE)
- **SBOM**: Software Bill of Materials
- **SCC**: Standard Contractual Clauses (GDPR transfer mechanism)
- **SDF**: Significant Data Fiduciary (DPDP)
- **SDLC**: Software Development Lifecycle
- **SEBI**: Securities and Exchange Board of India
- **SEC**: Securities and Exchange Commission (US)
- **SEG**: Secure Email Gateway
- **SEV**: Severity (incident classification)
- **SIEM**: Security Information and Event Management
- **SIG**: Standardised Information Gathering questionnaire (Shared Assessments)
- **SLA**: Service Level Agreement
- **SLE**: Single Loss Expectancy
- **SLSA**: Supply-chain Levels for Software Artifacts
- **SOAR**: Security Orchestration, Automation and Response
- **SoA**: Statement of Applicability (ISO 27001) (the first document an ISO auditor opens; if it is out of date everything downstream looks weak)
- **SOC**: System and Organization Controls (AICPA); also Security Operations Centre (one is a report, one is a team; do not confuse them on a sales call)
- **SOX**: Sarbanes-Oxley Act (US)
- **SPDI**: Sensitive Personal Data or Information (Indian IT Rules 2011)
- **SSAE**: Statements on Standards for Attestation Engagements (AICPA)
- **SSCP**: Systems Security Certified Practitioner (ISC2)
- **SSL / TLS**: Secure Sockets Layer / Transport Layer Security
- **SSO**: Single Sign-On
- **STRIDE**: Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information disclosure, Denial of service, Elevation of privilege

## T

- **TCP**: Transmission Control Protocol
- **TDPSA**: Texas Data Privacy and Security Act
- **TIA**: Transfer Impact Assessment (post-Schrems II)
- **TISAX**: Trusted Information Security Assessment Exchange (automotive)
- **TOTP**: Time-based One-Time Password
- **TPRM**: Third-Party Risk Management
- **TRAI**: Telecom Regulatory Authority of India
- **TSC**: Trust Services Criteria (SOC 2)

## U

- **UCPA**: Utah Consumer Privacy Act

## V

- **VCDPA**: Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act
- **VPN**: Virtual Private Network

## W

- **WAF**: Web Application Firewall
- **WebAuthn**: Web Authentication API (FIDO2)

## X / Z

- **XDR**: Extended Detection and Response
- **XSS**: Cross-Site Scripting
- **ZT / ZTA / ZTMM**: Zero Trust / Zero Trust Architecture / Zero Trust Maturity Model (CISA)
