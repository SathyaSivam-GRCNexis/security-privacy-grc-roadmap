# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be a serious adult. Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume good faith until repeatedly proven otherwise.

If that is enough for you, you can stop reading.

---

## The longer version

This repository attracts a wide audience: students, career-changers, working practitioners, hiring managers, regulators-in-training, and the occasional academic. The technical subjects we cover (security, privacy, governance, risk, compliance) involve real disagreements among reasonable, experienced people. Disagreement is welcome; it is how the field improves.

What is not welcome is anything that makes the space hostile to people who are trying to learn or contribute in good faith.

### Expected behaviour

- **Engage with the work, not the person.** Critique a control mapping, a regulatory interpretation, or a writing choice. Do not critique the contributor's intelligence, background, or motives.
- **Explain your reasoning.** A drive-by "this is wrong" without explanation does not help anyone. Cite, link, or walk through your thinking.
- **Be patient with beginners.** This repository exists in part to lower the barrier to entry. Questions that seem obvious to you are not obvious to a non-tech beginner two weeks into Module 1.
- **Be honest about uncertainty.** GRC is full of areas where the right answer depends on jurisdiction, sector, or risk appetite. Saying "it depends, and here is why" is more useful than overclaiming.
- **Respect time.** Maintainers are doing this in addition to other work. Patience with response times is appreciated.

### Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, harassment, or insults of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour relating to race, ethnicity, national origin, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, religion, or socio-economic background.
- Sustained disruption of discussions; bad-faith argumentation; sealioning.
- Public or private harassment of any participant.
- Posting other people's private information without permission.
- Promoting products, services, or employers in a way that drowns out genuine discussion.
- Submitting AI-generated content while claiming it as your own original work.

### Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaches this code, contact the maintainer privately via [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sathya-sivam) or by opening a confidential discussion if the platform supports it.

Reports will be acknowledged within a reasonable time, treated confidentially, and assessed on the evidence. The maintainer reserves the right to remove comments, close issues, block contributors, or take other action proportionate to the breach.

### Scope

This code applies to all spaces associated with the repository: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any direct communication arising from work on the repository. It also applies, with proportionality, to public conduct that materially affects the repository's community.

### Attribution

This Code of Conduct is influenced by the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) and adapted to fit the tone and audience of this repository.
